Put these files here before running the bot:

1. logo.png            -> your page's logo, transparent PNG works best
2. background.jpg       -> optional custom background photo (1080x1350 ideal)
3. fonts/DejaVuSans-Bold.ttf and fonts/DejaVuSans.ttf
   -> or any other .ttf fonts, just update config.py to point at them
   -> good free fonts: https://fonts.google.com (download the .ttf files)

If logo.png / background.jpg are missing, the template still works —
it just uses a plain navy background with no logo.
